Hybrid delivery boundary

HTTPS works even when P2P does not

HyperBEAM coordinates membership and the playback descriptor. Viewers exchange encoded HLS segments over WebRTC data channels when reachable and immediately fall back to ordinary HTTPS on port 443 when they are not. Connected P2P peers can see ICE-derived network addresses, so sharing remains an optional optimization.
